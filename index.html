Research Highlights

I work in artificial intelligence, aiming to build a new generation of embodied 3D intelligence that reasons, interacts, and adapts. Specifically, my research integrates 3D vision with reasoning to understand the 3D world, embodied AI for planning and action to interact with it, and test-time learning to continuously adapt world models and policies through experience. I like to think of this in terms of neuroscientist Anil Seth's five perspectives of selfhood:
①
②
  • Perspectival self (①): Building 3D foundation models and world models for perceiving and understanding the 3D physical world. 3D-LLM, 3D-CLR, 3D-CG etc.
  • Bodily self (②): Embodied reasoning for robots, acting through and reasoning about a physical body situated in 3D space. 3D-VLA, MultiPLY etc.
  • Volitional self (①↔②): Closing the perception-action loop, knowing how perception changes as a function of action. The sense of urges to act and of being the cause of what happens. ESI-Bench, Embodied Web Agents.
  • Narrative self (Emerged from countless perception-action ①↔② loops): Life, Experience and Long-Term Memory of a continuous and distinctive agent, accumulating a history of experience across episodes over perception-action loops rather than starting fresh each time. SlowFast-VGen, 3DLLM-Mem, Test-Time Planning, T2-z, T²Mem, I cover more of this in my blog, Three Levels of TTT.
  • Social self (Parallel ①↔② loops): Refracted through the minds of others, shaped by our social surroundings. We have some ongoing and future works on this, stay tuned!

As A Musician

I also work as a professional musician. I was hired to tour with PRXZM to SF, LA, Portland, Seattle and more as synth player and backup singer. In addition to my weekly job as a weekend organist, I occasionally tour within California with my band, Westerly. I was CVPR 2026 Social Chair, organizing the CVPR reception and musical performances, and performing as guitarist, synth keyboardist, banjoist, singer and percussionist.

Churches I've worked for for Sunday services / weddings / memorials: First Baptist Church of Glendale; Congregational Church of Sunnyvale; Sunnyvale Presbyterian Church; Stanford Memorial Church; Asbury United Methodist Church Livermore; First Pres Burlingame; SEA Episcopal Church in San Bruno; First Congregational Church of San Jose and more.



Outside of research, I'm a multi-instrumentalist, composer, and metalhead. I was the featured organist, keyboard section leader, pipa soloist, ondes martenot soloist in an orchestra (ondes martenot is a very rare instrument - only 60 ondes in total worldwide). I also play(ed) the pipe organ in churches; the keyboard and guitar in a rock band; occasionally play the saxophone, melodica, drums, harp, Konghou, in casual jams. (Favorite book. Favorite Band: Dream Theater. Favorite Album: Metropolis Pt 2.) Right now I'm really into synthesizers (all kinds!) and launchpads :)

I've been playing the piano since I was five, and I was the president of the piano association in Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I can do fast sightreading on staff / lead sheet, and have rich recording / performing experience on piano, organ and pipa. I'm a Young Organist of American Guild of Organists and my organ teacher was Prof. Christoph Bull from UCLA. I also have perfect pitch not just for notes but for chords.
